About Port AI Engineers

Structured Plant Engineering & CAD Delivery

Port AI Engineers Pvt. Ltd. is an ISO 9001:2015 certified engineering and CAD design services company headquartered in Hyderabad, India.

We support EPC contractors, industrial plant owners, engineering consultants, and equipment manufacturers with dedicated process engineering, 3D piping design, pipe stress analysis, and fabrication-ready CAD documentation.

Every engagement follows structured review checklists and standardized drafting automation so deliverables arrive coordinated across process, piping, instrumentation, and layout disciplines—reducing downstream site rework.

Enquiry Guidance & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions Before Requesting a Quote

How quickly will Port AI Engineers respond to my enquiry?+

You will receive an initial technical response within 24 working hours. For urgent discussions, you can also book a Google Calendar slot or reach us directly via phone or WhatsApp.

What details help prepare a project quote?+

Share your required discipline (e.g., P&ID, PFD, 3D Piping, Isometrics, GA Drawings, Stress Analysis, or Plant Layout), approximate sheet or line count, target CAD/stress platform, and project schedule. No initial file upload is needed on this form.

Can we sign an NDA and review sample drawings before starting?+

Yes. Anonymized engineering sample documentation is available upon request, subject to confidentiality and NDA review. Once we receive your enquiry, our team arranges a secure sharing and walkthrough process.

Which CAD and pipe stress software platforms do you deliver in?+

Our engineering team works natively in AutoCAD, AutoCAD Plant 3D, SP3D, E3D, PDMS, CADWORX, CAESAR II, and AutoPIPE, delivering outputs aligned with your project conventions.

Do you work with EPC contractors, consultants, and plants outside Hyderabad?+

Yes. While our engineering operations center is in Hyderabad, India, we collaborate remotely with EPC contractors, plant owners, engineering consultants, and industrial manufacturers across India and international regions.
